Every rupee, debited somewhere and credited somewhere else.

arthik is a self-hosted personal finance app with the everyday feel of a phone money tracker and the rigour of double-entry bookkeeping. It installs as an app on your phone, works offline, and keeps your data as plain, readable files you own.

Try the demo Sign in

The demo is read-only and full of made-up data; its user name and password are shown on the sign-in screen.

🏦 Accounts

Bank, cash, wallet, investments, deposits, property, credit cards and loans — balances to the paisa, cleared vs projected, credit-card limits, statements and due dates, and reconciliation.

🗂️ Categories

Two-level income and expense categories with totals for any week, month, quarter, half-year, year or custom range. A “both ways” kind covers things like market gains and losses.

🧾 Transactions

Expense, income, transfer and split entries, with a calculator amount field, remembered items, labels, notes and cleared/reconciled status. Past dates fix history; future dates post themselves on the day.

⏰ Scheduled

Recurring bills, salary, SIPs and EMIs — daily to yearly or every N days/weeks/months — posted automatically, with skip, pause, end date or count, and month-end dates that never drift.

🎯 Budgets

Weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly budgets per category, scaled to the period you view and reset each period, with progress against the calendar.

📊 Reports

Cash flow, spending and income by category with drill-down, category trends, net worth over time, balance sheet, account flow, labels and a calendar view. Every chart has a table.

🔍 Search & export

Search titles, notes and amounts, filter by anything, act on many transactions at once, and export exactly what you filtered as CSV — which imports straight back.

📱 Offline PWA

Install it on your phone. Add transactions with no signal — they sync when you are back online. Comfortable on a laptop too.

📄 Plain files

Data lives in YAML .md files next to your notes. Edit them in any app; arthik notices and re-syncs every balance and total within seconds.

What it stores

A grocery run of ₹540 paid by UPI is one balanced transaction: the wallet is credited, the category is debited.

- id: t-20261001-55c7e6 date: "2026-10-01" type: expense title: Groceries status: cleared postings: - account: wallet amount: '-540.00' - category: food-groceries amount: '540.00'
